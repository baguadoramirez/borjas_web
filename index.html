Borja Aguado

Welcome to my personal webpage. I share updates on projects, interests, and interactive experiments in perception and action.

My research focuses on 3D motion and time-to-contact, with a strong interest in new methodologies for measuring key components of the perception/action cycle and their potential applications, as well as clear data visualization and reproducible workflows.

If you are a coding enthusiast or a visualization aficionado, you will find resources, tools, and demos here.

Feel free to reach out by email with questions, suggestions, or just to say hello.
